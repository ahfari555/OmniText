Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

At OmniText, protecting your privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information collected and recorded by OmniText and how we use it.

Client-Side Processing (Zero Data Upload)

All text transformations, formatting, and counting operations are executed locally inside your web browser. Your text content is never uploaded or transmitted to any remote server.

Google AdSense & Cookies

We use third-party vendors, including Google, to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Ads Settings.
