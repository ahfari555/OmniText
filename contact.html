Have a question about the text tools, found an issue, or want to suggest an improvement to a guide? You can contact the OmniText team by email.

We aim to respond within 24–48 business hours. Response times may vary depending on the volume and nature of requests.

Helpful details to include

  • The URL of the page where you noticed the issue.
  • Your browser and device type, if relevant.
  • A short description of the problem and the steps to reproduce it.
