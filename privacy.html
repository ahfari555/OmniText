Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how OmniText handles information when you visit and use the website at omnitext-online.vercel.app.

Text you enter into the tools

The core text analysis, formatting, and rule-based writing review features are designed to process the text you enter within your browser. OmniText does not operate an account-based text storage service for these features, and the text entered into these tools is not intentionally submitted to an OmniText application server for processing.

The Markdown editor also runs in the browser. Its preview uses third-party JavaScript libraries delivered through external content-delivery services. Your browser may request those libraries from their providers when the page loads. Do not use the tools for highly confidential or sensitive material unless you are comfortable with the website and browser environment in which you are working.

Information collected through website operations

When you visit a website, the hosting provider and infrastructure used to deliver it may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, timestamps, and diagnostic logs. These records are generally used to deliver, secure, and maintain the website. The precise information and retention periods depend on the relevant service providers and their settings.

Cookies, advertising, and Google AdSense

OmniText may use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to store or access information on your device, measure advertising, prevent fraud, and, where permitted by your settings and applicable requirements, personalize ads. Advertising technologies may process identifiers, IP address, browser information, and information about interactions with ads or pages.

You can review Google's information about advertising technologies and manage available ad personalization settings through Google Ads Settings. The availability of controls may depend on your location and account status.

Third-party services

The website may load resources such as fonts, CSS or JavaScript libraries, hosting services, and advertising scripts from third-party providers. Those providers may receive technical request information when your browser connects to them. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

How information is used

  • To provide and maintain the website and its tools.
  • To diagnose errors, protect the website, and prevent abuse.
  • To understand website performance where measurement tools are enabled.
  • To display and measure advertising where advertising is enabled.
  • To respond to messages sent to our contact email.

Children's privacy

OmniText is a general-purpose website and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us so we can review the request.

Policy changes

We may update this policy when the website, its tools, or applicable requirements change. The updated date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.
